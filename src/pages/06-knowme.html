How it works

  1. Enter both names.
  2. Each round shows a prompt about one of you. The other types a guess and locks it in.
  3. The person the question is about says the real answer and scores the guess: two points for nailed it, one for close.
  4. After ten rounds, each of you has a score out of ten.

A wrong guess is not a failure. It is a conversation: “Really? Since when?” is where the good stories start.
