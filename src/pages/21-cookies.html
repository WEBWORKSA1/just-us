Advertising cookies

Advertising on Just.us is served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may set and read cookies or use similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad and, where you have agreed, personalise ads based on your visits to this and other sites. Common examples include cookies set from the doubleclick.net and google.com domains.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message lets you accept, refuse or manage advertising cookies, and you can change your choice at any time from that message.
  • Anywhere, you can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.
  • Your browser can block or delete cookies and clear local storage. The site still works; your saved items and streak will reset.

Other services

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Video thumbnails load from YouTube, and the YouTube player, which may set its own cookies, loads only after you press play. More detail is in the privacy policy.
