Making a list you will finish

  • Mix sizes. A few big trips, plenty of things you could do this month.
  • Both add. Aim for an even split, including a few that are only for the other person’s sake.
  • Schedule one. A list only works if something leaves it. Pick the next one each month.

Your list is stored in this browser only. Use “Copy the list” to send it to your partner or keep a backup.
