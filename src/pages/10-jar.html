How to use a date jar

  1. Print the slips and cut along the dashed lines.
  2. Fold each one and drop it in any jar, mug or box.
  3. Draw one on the same day each week. No swaps, apart from one veto a month each.
  4. Keep the used slips in an envelope. By next year it is a record of everything you did.
