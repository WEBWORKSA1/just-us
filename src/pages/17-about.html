What this is

Just.us exists for the moment one of you asks “what shall we do tonight?” and the other says “I don’t mind”. It gives you a date that fits your evening, a question worth answering, or a ten-minute game, without an app to install, an account to create or a subscription to cancel.

The name is the domain: just, dot, us. It is meant literally. Phones down, door closed, just the two of you.

How we write

Everything original on the site is written and edited for Just.us. We keep it family-friendly, inclusive of every kind of couple and close pair, and practical: each guide ends with something to do tonight. Where we mention research, we describe it cautiously and name the researchers. We are not therapists and nothing here is counselling; if you are struggling or feel unsafe, please contact a qualified professional or a local support service.

The reading list and videos are gathered automatically from publishers we respect. Headlines and videos belong to their owners and always link to the original.

Privacy by design

Your answers, scores, saved dates and lists stay in your own browser. We do not run user accounts. The only personal details we receive are the ones you choose to send through a form. The privacy policy has the detail.

How it is paid for

The site is free to use. It is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense, clearly labelled sponsorships, and reader support. Advertisers and sponsors have no say over editorial content.
