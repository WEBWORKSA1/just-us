The five care styles

  • Kind words: appreciation and encouragement, said or written.
  • Undivided time: full attention and shared activity.
  • Practical help: tasks handled, problems eased.
  • Thoughtful gestures: small tokens that show someone was thinking of you.
  • Physical closeness: hugs, hand-holding, being near.

Most people respond to all five; the quiz shows which matter most to you right now. Results can shift with circumstances, so it is worth retaking once or twice a year.
