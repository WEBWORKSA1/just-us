Advertise and partner

People arrive here with one question: what shall we do together? If your venue, product or service is a good answer, there are several ways to be in front of them.

Ways to work with us

Daily page sponsorYour name on Today for Two, the most-revisited page on the site, for a week or a month.
Category or deck sponsorOwn a relevant context: a date category such as Foodie or Outdoor, or a question deck.
Venue and experience listingsRestaurants, classes, stays and activities, recommended in custom date plans for your area.
Contest prize partnerProvide the monthly prize and be credited on the contest page and in the winner announcement.
Newsletter sponsorOne clearly labelled placement in The Friday Two.
Content partnershipCo-created guides, decks or tools for brands whose customers are couples.

Our standards

  • Sponsored placements are always labelled as such.
  • We keep the site family-friendly and decline adult, gambling and misleading offers.
  • Editorial picks cannot be bought. Sponsors appear alongside them, not instead of them.
  • Display advertising elsewhere on the site is served by Google AdSense under its own policies.
