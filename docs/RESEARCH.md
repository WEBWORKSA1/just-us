## What the field does, and what Just.us does about it

1. **Lists dominate; tools are rare.** Fourteen of sixteen date publishers ship a static list. Only one offered filters and only three small tools generate a pick. Just.us leads with a generator (place, budget, time, mood), three vetoes, repeat prevention and a matched question, and still publishes every idea as crawlable HTML.
2. **The best mechanic is locked in apps.** Hidden-until-both-answer and streaks sit behind app installs and $30-$70 a year. Just.us does both in the browser on one shared phone, free.
3. **Category pages match search intent.** Winners split by situation (at home, free, rainy, long-distance, anniversary, first date). Just.us builds 18 category pages and 12 deck pages from two text files.
4. **Printables capture leads.** Date-jar printables are the most common lead magnet. Just.us gives the jar away as a tool and captures leads where intent is highest: a three-step custom date-plan form.
5. **Quizzes funnel.** Free quiz, optional paid report or account. Just.us uses an original care-style quiz with a share-to-compare link that brings the partner in.
6. **Trust signals.** Clinician credits, cited research, "why trust us", safety notices. Just.us names researchers, states plainly that it is not counselling and links safety help.
7. **Monetization mix.** Ads plus affiliate plus one owned product. Just.us: AdSense Auto ads, labelled sponsorship slots, venue leads from date plans, reader support, sponsor-funded contest prizes.
8. **Freshness.** Few sites change daily. Just.us publishes a dated daily page, an RSS feed, and auto-refreshed reads and videos every six hours.
