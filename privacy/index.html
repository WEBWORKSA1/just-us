Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026. Plain version: we do not run accounts, your game answers stay on your device, and we only receive what you send us through a form.

Who we are

Just.us (“we”, “us”) is an independent website published at this domain. You can reach us through the contact form.

What stays on your device

Answers to the daily question, quiz results, game scores, player names, saved date ideas, your days-together date and your bucket list are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are never transmitted to us and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data removes them. Quiz results travel only inside a link you choose to share.

What you send us

When you submit a form (newsletter, date plan, contact, partner enquiry, support pledge, contest entry or talent application) we receive the details you typed, the page you sent it from and the time. Forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service, which processes the submission on our behalf under its own privacy policy. We use these details only for the purpose you sent them: to reply, send what you asked for, run the contest or consider your application. We include third-party offers in a date plan, or send the newsletter, only if you ticked the box asking for it.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertising. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and out of some other vendors’ personalised ads at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown. See also our cookie policy.

Other third parties

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations.
  • Fonts: typefaces load from Google Fonts.
  • Videos: video thumbnails load from YouTube; the YouTube player, in its privacy-enhanced mode, loads only when you press play.
  • Links: articles, videos and partner pages open on other websites with their own policies.

How long we keep things

Enquiries and applications are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for up to 24 months afterwards. Contest entries are kept until the contest is settled, and winning stories for as long as they are published. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents may ask what personal information we hold and ask us to delete it; we do not sell personal information. To exercise any right, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

Children

Just.us is written for adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us details, tell us and we will delete them.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
