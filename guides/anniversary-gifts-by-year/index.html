Anniversary gifts by year, and what to do instead of buying one

By the Just.us editors · Updated November 10, 2026 · 3 min read

The custom of matching each wedding anniversary to a material is more than a century old, and the best-known list runs from fragile to precious: the idea being that a marriage, like the gifts, grows sturdier with time. You do not have to be married, or to buy anything, to borrow the idea. Each material makes a good theme for a date.

The traditional list

YearTraditional giftDo this instead
1stPaperWrite letters to open on your second anniversary.
2ndCottonA picnic on a new blanket you will keep using.
3rdLeatherA weekend away, with a luggage tag that stays on the bag.
4thFruit or flowersA botanical garden, or a morning picking your own fruit.
5thWoodA cabin night, or plant a tree together.
10thTinFill a tin as a time capsule for your twentieth.
15thCrystalGood glasses and a tasting flight at home.
20thChinaCook a proper dinner and use the plates you save for guests.
25thSilverRecreate your first date, twenty-five years on.
30thPearlA trip to the coast.
40thRubyA long dinner with the people who were there at the start.
50thGoldGather everyone, and tell the story from both sides.

Lists vary a little between countries and some modern versions substitute other items, so treat it as inspiration, not a rule.

What makes an anniversary feel like one

  • Look back. Each choose three moments from the past year and say why. The memories deck helps.
  • Look ahead. Name one thing you each want from the year to come.
  • Mark it physically. A photo in the same spot every year, a line added to a shared notebook, a letter sealed for next time.

Count your own milestones

Not married, or more interested in day one than the wedding day? The days-together calculator shows how long it has been and when your next round-number day falls. Day 1,000 deserves a dinner as much as any anniversary.
