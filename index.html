Questions people ask

Is Just.us really free?

Yes. Every tool, deck and guide is free to use with no account. The site is paid for by advertising, sponsors and reader support.

Do we both need the app or an account?

There is no app and no account. Everything runs in the browser on one device that you pass back and forth, or on two devices with a shared link.

Where do our answers go?

Nowhere. Answers to the daily question, saved dates, scores and lists are stored only in your own browser. Forms you choose to send us, such as a date-plan request, are the only things that leave your device.

Is this only for romantic couples?

No. Most of the questions and games work for any two people who want to know each other better: partners, best friends, siblings, a parent and a grown-up child.

Is this relationship advice?

No. Just.us offers ideas and prompts for fun and general information. If you are worried about your relationship or your safety, please speak to a qualified professional or a local support service.
