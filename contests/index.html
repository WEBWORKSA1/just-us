The monthly story contest

Every month has a theme. Tell us a short, true story that fits it. The best one wins a prize and is published on the site.

Theme for November 2026

The kindest small thing my person does

Prize: a US$50 date-night gift card and a feature on the site.

Closes: November 30, 2026, 23:59 UTC ·

How it works

  1. Write up to 300 words about something that really happened to the two of you.
  2. Send it with the form before the closing date. One entry per person each month.
  3. The editors choose a winner within fourteen days of closing, judging on originality, warmth and how useful the story is to other couples.
  4. The winner is contacted by email and the story is published with first names only.

Rules

  • No purchase or payment is needed to enter or win. Supporting the site does not improve your chances.
  • Open to adults aged 18 or over. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • Entries must be your own, true, family-friendly and not published elsewhere. Do not include other people’s private details.
  • The prize is as described above, or an equivalent of the same value where a gift card is unavailable in the winner’s country. No cash alternative unless we offer one. Any taxes are the winner’s responsibility.
  • If a winner does not reply within fourteen days, another entry may be chosen. If no entry meets the standard, the prize may roll over.
  • By entering you allow Just.us to edit lightly and publish your story on the site and its channels, credited by first name. You keep the copyright.
  • The editors’ decision is final. Details you submit are used only to run the contest, as set out in the privacy policy.

Brands and venues: sponsor next month’s prize.

Entry received

Good luck. If your story wins, we will email you within fourteen days of the closing date.
