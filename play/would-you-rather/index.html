Would-you-rather questions for couples

Prefer to read them out? Here are thirty from the game.

  1. Would you rather have dinner under the stars or have breakfast in bed?
  2. Would you rather plan every detail of a trip or show up at the airport and pick a flight?
  3. Would you rather get a handwritten letter or get a surprise day out?
  4. Would you rather spend a weekend in a cabin in the woods or spend it in a hotel in a big city?
  5. Would you rather cook together every night or eat out every night?
  6. Would you rather relive our first date or fast-forward to our fiftieth anniversary for one day?
  7. Would you rather have a home with a huge kitchen or have a home with a huge garden?
  8. Would you rather always know what I am thinking or always be able to make me laugh?
  9. Would you rather take a dance class together or take a cooking class together?
  10. Would you rather go on one big holiday a year or go on six small weekends away?
  11. Would you rather watch the sunrise together or watch the sunset together?
  12. Would you rather have a quiet night in or have a loud night out?
  13. Would you rather be able to pause time on good days or be able to rewind and redo awkward moments?
  14. Would you rather share one dessert or order one each and swap halfway?
  15. Would you rather live by the sea or live in the mountains?
  16. Would you rather receive one big present or receive lots of small ones?
  17. Would you rather have a personal chef or have a personal cleaner?
  18. Would you rather spend a rainy day with films or spend a rainy day with board games?
  19. Would you rather learn a language together or learn an instrument together?
  20. Would you rather travel back to see how our grandparents met or travel forward to meet our great-grandchildren?
  21. Would you rather have a dog or have a cat?
  22. Would you rather do karaoke in front of strangers or do a dance routine in front of friends?
  23. Would you rather get up early together or stay up late together?
  24. Would you rather own a tiny house we love or own a big house that needs work?
  25. Would you rather double-date with our closest friends or go out just the two of us?
  26. Would you rather have every meal be a picnic or have every meal be a candlelit dinner?
  27. Would you rather go camping or go to a spa?
  28. Would you rather always pick the film or always pick the restaurant?
  29. Would you rather have a slow Sunday at home or have a Sunday full of plans?
  30. Would you rather win a trip around the world or win our dream home?

The fun is in the why. After each round, give your reason in one sentence. When you split, each make the case for your side.
