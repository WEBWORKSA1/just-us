Fall date ideas

Crisp air, changing leaves and harvest season. Dates for sweaters, orchards and early evenings.

Fire pit or balcony s'mores

A fire pit if you have one, a candle and skewers if you do not. Sticky fingers and an excuse to sit outside in the dark.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Golden-hour walk with a flask

Check when the sun sets, leave forty minutes before, bring something hot to drink and take the long way round.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Hike to a view

Pick a trail that ends somewhere worth sitting. Pack a snack for the top and stay until one of you gets cold.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Pick-your-own fruit

Apples in autumn, berries in summer. Pick more than you need and turn the surplus into a pie or jam that evening.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Pumpkin patch and carving

Choose a pumpkin for each other, carve them at home and put both on the doorstep. Roast the seeds.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Flea market morning

Five dollars each to find the strangest object for the other. Coffee first; haggling is optional.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Photo walk

Choose a theme such as doors, dogs or one colour. Shoot on your phones for an hour and pick each other's best three.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Day trip to a town an hour away

Choose a small town within an hour, arrive with no plan and ask the first shopkeeper where to eat.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~360 min

Autumn leaves drive

Pick a scenic road, a playlist and a farm stand to stop at. Take turns choosing when to pull over.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~180 min
