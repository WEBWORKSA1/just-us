Adventurous date ideas

Try something neither of you has done. New experiences shared are the ones you will still talk about next year.

Map out your next visit

Open a shared map and pin what you will do when you are next in the same place. Anticipation is half the pleasure.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Hike to a view

Pick a trail that ends somewhere worth sitting. Pack a snack for the top and stay until one of you gets cold.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Thrift shop outfit challenge

Set a ten-dollar limit and choose a complete outfit for each other. Wear it to dinner. No vetoes.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Live music at a small venue

Pick a local act you have never heard of. Small rooms are cheap, loud in a good way and easy to leave if it is awful.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Comedy or open-mic night

Laughing in the same room does a lot of work for you. Sit a few rows back unless you want to be part of the act.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Tourists in your own city

Do the things visitors do and locals never get round to: the viewpoint, the walking tour, the famous sandwich.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Indoor climbing session

Most gyms offer a beginner session with shoes included. Cheering each other up a wall builds trust quickly.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Kayak, canoe or paddleboard

Rent by the hour on calm water. A double kayak is a famously honest test of how well you communicate.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Volunteer for a morning

A food bank shift, a park clean-up, a shelter walk. Doing something useful together is its own kind of closeness.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Drive out to see the stars

Get thirty minutes away from city lights, lie on the bonnet or a blanket and stay for one full hour.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Go back to where you met

Return to the place it started, or the nearest version of it. Retell the story from both sides.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Flea market morning

Five dollars each to find the strangest object for the other. Coffee first; haggling is optional.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Escape room

Sixty minutes, one locked room and a clear view of how you each behave under pressure. Better with a second couple.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Aquarium or zoo visit

Plenty to look at and talk about, with no pressure to fill silences. Decide which animal each of you would be.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Scavenger hunt or geocaching

Use a free geocaching app or write each other a list of ten things to find in your neighbourhood.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Day trip to a town an hour away

Choose a small town within an hour, arrive with no plan and ask the first shopkeeper where to eat.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~360 min

Tour open houses

Spend a weekend afternoon wandering homes for sale. Decide where the sofa would go and who gets the good wardrobe.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Ferry or train ride to nowhere

Take a cheap scenic route to the end of the line, have a coffee and come straight back. The ride is the date.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Sunrise breakfast picnic

Set an alarm, bring pastries and a flask and watch the day start from somewhere with a view. Nap later.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Late-night diner run

Go out after ten for pie, chips or pancakes in a booth. Ordinary food feels like an occasion at that hour.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Fifteen-dollar dinner challenge

Fifteen dollars and fifteen minutes in the supermarket to shop for a three-course meal. Cook it together afterwards.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Hotel night in your own town

Book one night somewhere nearby with a good breakfast. A different bed and no chores change the whole mood.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~720 min
