Anniversary date ideas

Ways to mark another year: some look back at how you got here, some are simply worth dressing up for.

Blanket fort film night

Build a proper fort from chairs and sheets, then watch a film from the year you met. Snacks are compulsory.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~150 min

Memory lane slideshow

Each of you picks ten photos from your time together and explains why. Cast them to the big screen if you can.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Letters to open in a year

Write each other a letter about right now: what you love, what you hope for. Seal them and set a reminder for next year.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Fondue night

Cheese first, chocolate after. Eating slowly from one pot forces a long, unhurried dinner conversation.

  • $$
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Recreate your first date at home

Same food, same music, same outfits if they still fit. Compare what each of you remembers; the versions never quite match.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Build the playlist of us

Add songs in order: the one from when you met, the road-trip one, the one from the hard month. Tell the story of each.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Make a time capsule

A shoebox, today's receipts, a photo, a note each, predictions for five years from now. Tape it shut and date it.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Vision board for the year ahead

Old magazines or a shared online board. Build one for the two of you: places, habits, a project, something just for fun.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Open a surprise delivery together

Each sends the other a small parcel timed to arrive the same week. Open them on a call, at the same moment.

  • $$
  • At home
  • ~30 min

Take a class together

Pottery, pasta, glass-blowing, knife skills. Being beginners side by side is a quick way to see each other fresh.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Botanical garden stroll

Slow, pretty and built for wandering. Each of you chooses the plant that most resembles the other, and explains.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Drive out to see the stars

Get thirty minutes away from city lights, lie on the bonnet or a blanket and stay for one full hour.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Dress up for a proper dinner

Book the place you keep saying is for a special occasion. Order the thing you cannot make at home, and dessert.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Go back to where you met

Return to the place it started, or the nearest version of it. Retell the story from both sides.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Day trip to a town an hour away

Choose a small town within an hour, arrive with no plan and ask the first shopkeeper where to eat.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~360 min

Tasting flight

Wine, cider, tea, coffee or chocolate. Order a flight, taste slowly and write your own ridiculous tasting notes.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Hotel night in your own town

Book one night somewhere nearby with a good breakfast. A different bed and no chores change the whole mood.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~720 min
