Foodie date ideas

For couples who plan the day around the next meal: cook it, taste it, hunt it down or judge it together.

Cook a cuisine neither of you has tried

Pick a country, choose one recipe each, shop together and cook side by side. Score the result out of ten and keep the winner.

  • $$
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Blind taste test

Buy five versions of one thing, such as chocolate, crisps or cola. Blindfold each other, taste, rank, then reveal the labels.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Homemade pizza face-off

Same dough, separate toppings, one oven. Each of you builds a pizza in secret and the other has to judge it honestly.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Cocktail or mocktail lab

Use whatever is in the cupboard. Each of you invents a drink for the other, names it and writes the menu description.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Bake something ambitious

Choose a bake that is slightly beyond you both, like croissants or a layer cake. Success and failure are equally memorable.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~150 min

Fondue night

Cheese first, chocolate after. Eating slowly from one pot forces a long, unhurried dinner conversation.

  • $$
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Breakfast for dinner

Pancakes, eggs, the good coffee and pyjamas at seven in the evening. Low effort, high comfort.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Themed movie marathon

Pick a theme, such as one director or one decade, and cook snacks to match. Intermission walks keep it from becoming a nap.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~240 min

Dessert-only dinner

Skip the main course once. Three small desserts, shared, eaten slowly. Rank them and argue about the ranking.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Cook the same recipe on a call

Choose a simple recipe, shop separately, cook together on video and eat at the same table, five hundred miles apart.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Farmers' market challenge

Give yourselves ten dollars each and split up. Meet in twenty minutes and build lunch from whatever you both bought.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Picnic in a park you have never visited

Choose a green space on the map that neither of you knows. Simple food, a blanket and no plan for afterwards.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Coffee shop crawl

Three cafes, one small drink in each, a walk in between. Score them on coffee, chairs and people-watching.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Take a class together

Pottery, pasta, glass-blowing, knife skills. Being beginners side by side is a quick way to see each other fresh.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Dessert crawl

Skip dinner and visit three places for one shared dessert each. Walk between them and keep score.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Food truck or night market dinner

Order one thing each from different stalls and share everything. Standing up to eat makes it feel like a small festival.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Pick-your-own fruit

Apples in autumn, berries in summer. Pick more than you need and turn the surplus into a pie or jam that evening.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Pub or cafe quiz night

Join a quiz as a team of two. You will find out fast which of you knows capitals and which knows pop lyrics.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Dress up for a proper dinner

Book the place you keep saying is for a special occasion. Order the thing you cannot make at home, and dessert.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Tasting flight

Wine, cider, tea, coffee or chocolate. Order a flight, taste slowly and write your own ridiculous tasting notes.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Late-night diner run

Go out after ten for pie, chips or pancakes in a booth. Ordinary food feels like an occasion at that hour.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Fifteen-dollar dinner challenge

Fifteen dollars and fifteen minutes in the supermarket to shop for a three-course meal. Cook it together afterwards.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min
