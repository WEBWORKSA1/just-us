Creative date ideas

Make something side by side. Nobody needs talent; the point is the mess, the laughing and the thing you keep.

Cook a cuisine neither of you has tried

Pick a country, choose one recipe each, shop together and cook side by side. Score the result out of ten and keep the winner.

  • $$
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Paint each other's portrait

Set a twenty-minute timer and paint or draw each other. Swap at the end. The worse the portraits, the better the night.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Learn a dance from a video

Find a beginner swing, salsa or waltz tutorial, push the sofa back and get through one full song without stopping.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Cocktail or mocktail lab

Use whatever is in the cupboard. Each of you invents a drink for the other, names it and writes the menu description.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Bake something ambitious

Choose a bake that is slightly beyond you both, like croissants or a layer cake. Success and failure are equally memorable.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~150 min

Karaoke for two

Search any song plus the word karaoke. Each of you picks three for yourself and one the other person has to sing.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Build the playlist of us

Add songs in order: the one from when you met, the road-trip one, the one from the hard month. Tell the story of each.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Plant a windowsill herb garden

A few pots, some soil and three herbs you actually cook with. Name them. Share watering duty.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Trivia night about each other

Each of you writes ten quiz questions about your own life. Swap and answer. Works just as well over a video call.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Learn ten phrases in a new language

Pick a language tied to a trip you would like to take. Learn ten useful phrases and use them for the rest of the night.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Make a time capsule

A shoebox, today's receipts, a photo, a note each, predictions for five years from now. Tape it shut and date it.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Paper-folding challenge

Follow the same origami tutorial at the same time and compare results. Start with a crane; end with whatever happens.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Vision board for the year ahead

Old magazines or a shared online board. Build one for the two of you: places, habits, a project, something just for fun.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Virtual museum tour

Many major museums offer free online tours. Share a screen, wander and each choose the one piece you would take home.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Museum on a free day

Most museums have a free evening or day each month. Each of you picks a favourite piece and explains it badly.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Thrift shop outfit challenge

Set a ten-dollar limit and choose a complete outfit for each other. Wear it to dinner. No vetoes.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Take a class together

Pottery, pasta, glass-blowing, knife skills. Being beginners side by side is a quick way to see each other fresh.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Pumpkin patch and carving

Choose a pumpkin for each other, carve them at home and put both on the doorstep. Roast the seeds.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Photo walk

Choose a theme such as doors, dogs or one colour. Shoot on your phones for an hour and pick each other's best three.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Garden centre plant adoption

Each choose a plant for the other, name it and take joint custody. Low stakes, long-lasting souvenir.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Paint-your-own pottery

Paint a mug for each other. You collect them a week later, which builds in a second date.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Fifteen-dollar dinner challenge

Fifteen dollars and fifteen minutes in the supermarket to shop for a three-course meal. Cook it together afterwards.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Community centre class

Ballroom, ceramics, archery, improv. Local centres run one-off sessions cheaply, and you get to be new at it together.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Planetarium or science museum

Lean back under a dome of stars, then press every button in the hands-on gallery like the children do.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min
