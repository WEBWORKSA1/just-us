Group & double date ideas

Dates that are better with another couple or a few friends: a little competition, plenty to talk about afterwards.

Blind taste test

Buy five versions of one thing, such as chocolate, crisps or cola. Blindfold each other, taste, rank, then reveal the labels.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~45 min

Homemade pizza face-off

Same dough, separate toppings, one oven. Each of you builds a pizza in secret and the other has to judge it honestly.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Board game tournament

Best of three across three different games. Loser makes breakfast. Keep a running scoreboard on the fridge.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Cocktail or mocktail lab

Use whatever is in the cupboard. Each of you invents a drink for the other, names it and writes the menu description.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Karaoke for two

Search any song plus the word karaoke. Each of you picks three for yourself and one the other person has to sing.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Trivia night about each other

Each of you writes ten quiz questions about your own life. Swap and answer. Works just as well over a video call.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Mini golf

Silly enough that nobody can take it seriously. Invent a forfeit for every hole lost.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~75 min

Bowling night

Rented shoes level everyone. Bowl one round normally and one with your weaker hand.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Thrift shop outfit challenge

Set a ten-dollar limit and choose a complete outfit for each other. Wear it to dinner. No vetoes.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Live music at a small venue

Pick a local act you have never heard of. Small rooms are cheap, loud in a good way and easy to leave if it is awful.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Comedy or open-mic night

Laughing in the same room does a lot of work for you. Sit a few rows back unless you want to be part of the act.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Volunteer for a morning

A food bank shift, a park clean-up, a shelter walk. Doing something useful together is its own kind of closeness.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Dessert crawl

Skip dinner and visit three places for one shared dessert each. Walk between them and keep score.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Ice skating

Holding on to each other is practically required. Finish with something hot to drink while your feet recover.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Arcade night

Set a token budget, pool your tickets and choose one ridiculous prize together at the end.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Pub or cafe quiz night

Join a quiz as a team of two. You will find out fast which of you knows capitals and which knows pop lyrics.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Escape room

Sixty minutes, one locked room and a clear view of how you each behave under pressure. Better with a second couple.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Scavenger hunt or geocaching

Use a free geocaching app or write each other a list of ten things to find in your neighbourhood.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Roller skating

Loud music, questionable balance and a good reason to hold hands. Rinks often run cheap evening sessions.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Tasting flight

Wine, cider, tea, coffee or chocolate. Order a flight, taste slowly and write your own ridiculous tasting notes.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Racket sports in the park

Tennis, pickleball or badminton on a public court. Play one serious set and one where everything counts as in.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~60 min
