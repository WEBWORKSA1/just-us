Winter date ideas

Cold-weather dates, half of them out in the frost and half of them firmly under a blanket.

Living-room picnic

Blanket on the floor, finger food, a candle and both phones in another room. Surprisingly different from eating at the table.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Blanket fort film night

Build a proper fort from chairs and sheets, then watch a film from the year you met. Snacks are compulsory.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~150 min

Board game tournament

Best of three across three different games. Loser makes breakfast. Keep a running scoreboard on the fridge.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Puzzle and podcast night

A jigsaw on the table, a podcast you both like and nowhere to be. Talking happens sideways, which is often easiest.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Spa night at home

Face masks, a foot soak, a playlist and warm towels from the dryer. Take turns giving a ten-minute shoulder rub.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Bake something ambitious

Choose a bake that is slightly beyond you both, like croissants or a layer cake. Success and failure are equally memorable.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~150 min

Fondue night

Cheese first, chocolate after. Eating slowly from one pot forces a long, unhurried dinner conversation.

  • $$
  • At home
  • ~90 min

No-screens candlelit evening

Switch off everything with a screen for one evening. Candles, cards, talking. Notice how long the night suddenly feels.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~120 min

Themed movie marathon

Pick a theme, such as one director or one decade, and cook snacks to match. Intermission walks keep it from becoming a nap.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~240 min

Vision board for the year ahead

Old magazines or a shared online board. Build one for the two of you: places, habits, a project, something just for fun.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~90 min

Coffee shop crawl

Three cafes, one small drink in each, a walk in between. Score them on coffee, chairs and people-watching.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Ice skating

Holding on to each other is practically required. Finish with something hot to drink while your feet recover.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Holiday lights walk

Find the most decorated streets nearby and judge every house. Hot chocolate in a flask, gloves, slow pace.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Sledding or snowman building

The first decent snowfall, a hill, something to slide on. Build a snowman of each other afterwards.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Hot chocolate walk

Buy the best hot chocolate nearby and walk until the cups are empty. Short, warm and easy to extend.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~45 min
