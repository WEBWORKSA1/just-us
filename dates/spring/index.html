Spring date ideas

Longer evenings, markets reopening, things in bloom. Dates for the first warm weeks of the year.

Plant a windowsill herb garden

A few pots, some soil and three herbs you actually cook with. Name them. Share watering duty.

  • $
  • At home
  • ~60 min

Golden-hour walk with a flask

Check when the sun sets, leave forty minutes before, bring something hot to drink and take the long way round.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Farmers' market challenge

Give yourselves ten dollars each and split up. Meet in twenty minutes and build lunch from whatever you both bought.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Hike to a view

Pick a trail that ends somewhere worth sitting. Pack a snack for the top and stay until one of you gets cold.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Picnic in a park you have never visited

Choose a green space on the map that neither of you knows. Simple food, a blanket and no plan for afterwards.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Bike ride with a snack stop

Pick a bakery or ice-cream place a few miles away and make it the destination. The ride home is the dessert tax.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Botanical garden stroll

Slow, pretty and built for wandering. Each of you chooses the plant that most resembles the other, and explains.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Flea market morning

Five dollars each to find the strangest object for the other. Coffee first; haggling is optional.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Fly a kite

A cheap kite, a windy hill and twenty minutes of getting it wrong before it finally lifts.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Garden centre plant adoption

Each choose a plant for the other, name it and take joint custody. Low stakes, long-lasting souvenir.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Sunrise breakfast picnic

Set an alarm, bring pastries and a flask and watch the day start from somewhere with a view. Nap later.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min
