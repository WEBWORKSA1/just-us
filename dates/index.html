Date ideas, picked for tonight

Tell the generator what tonight allows. It picks one date, with a question to ask on the way. Do not like it? You get three vetoes.

Cost guide: Free, $ under about US$20 for two, $$ about US$20–60, $$$ more than that.

Saved for later

How couples use this: one person sets the filters, the other presses spin. Whoever did not veto last time plans the details.
