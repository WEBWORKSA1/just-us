First & early date ideas

Low-pressure first date ideas and early-days dates where talking is easy, leaving is easy and nobody has to perform.

Two truths and a dream

Each of you shares two true stories and one thing you wish had happened. The other guesses which is the dream.

  • Free
  • At home
  • ~30 min

Farmers' market challenge

Give yourselves ten dollars each and split up. Meet in twenty minutes and build lunch from whatever you both bought.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Picnic in a park you have never visited

Choose a green space on the map that neither of you knows. Simple food, a blanket and no plan for afterwards.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Bookshop date

Separate for fifteen minutes and each choose a book the other should read. Explain your choice over coffee.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Museum on a free day

Most museums have a free evening or day each month. Each of you picks a favourite piece and explains it badly.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Mini golf

Silly enough that nobody can take it seriously. Invent a forfeit for every hole lost.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~75 min

Bowling night

Rented shoes level everyone. Bowl one round normally and one with your weaker hand.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Coffee shop crawl

Three cafes, one small drink in each, a walk in between. Score them on coffee, chairs and people-watching.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Take a class together

Pottery, pasta, glass-blowing, knife skills. Being beginners side by side is a quick way to see each other fresh.

  • $$$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Live music at a small venue

Pick a local act you have never heard of. Small rooms are cheap, loud in a good way and easy to leave if it is awful.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Comedy or open-mic night

Laughing in the same room does a lot of work for you. Sit a few rows back unless you want to be part of the act.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Tourists in your own city

Do the things visitors do and locals never get round to: the viewpoint, the walking tour, the famous sandwich.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~180 min

Indoor climbing session

Most gyms offer a beginner session with shoes included. Cheering each other up a wall builds trust quickly.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Dessert crawl

Skip dinner and visit three places for one shared dessert each. Walk between them and keep score.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Botanical garden stroll

Slow, pretty and built for wandering. Each of you chooses the plant that most resembles the other, and explains.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Food truck or night market dinner

Order one thing each from different stalls and share everything. Standing up to eat makes it feel like a small festival.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Ice skating

Holding on to each other is practically required. Finish with something hot to drink while your feet recover.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Arcade night

Set a token budget, pool your tickets and choose one ridiculous prize together at the end.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Pub or cafe quiz night

Join a quiz as a team of two. You will find out fast which of you knows capitals and which knows pop lyrics.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Photo walk

Choose a theme such as doors, dogs or one colour. Shoot on your phones for an hour and pick each other's best three.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Aquarium or zoo visit

Plenty to look at and talk about, with no pressure to fill silences. Decide which animal each of you would be.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~150 min

Paint-your-own pottery

Paint a mug for each other. You collect them a week later, which builds in a second date.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Roller skating

Loud music, questionable balance and a good reason to hold hands. Rinks often run cheap evening sessions.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~90 min

Ferry or train ride to nowhere

Take a cheap scenic route to the end of the line, have a coffee and come straight back. The ride is the date.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~120 min

Dog park visit

Bring your own dog or simply go to watch. Award prizes for best ears, worst behaviour and most like you.

  • Free
  • Out
  • ~60 min

Hot chocolate walk

Buy the best hot chocolate nearby and walk until the cups are empty. Short, warm and easy to extend.

  • $
  • Out
  • ~45 min

Planetarium or science museum

Lean back under a dome of stars, then press every button in the hands-on gallery like the children do.

  • $$
  • Out
  • ~120 min
