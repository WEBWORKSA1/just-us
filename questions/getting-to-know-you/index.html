Getting-to-know-you questions for new couples

Easy openers for the early days. They are light enough for a second date and revealing enough to tell you who you are sitting across from.

All 25 questions in this deck

  1. What is a small thing that reliably makes your day better?
  2. Which meal could you eat every week and never get bored of?
  3. What did you want to be when you were ten?
  4. What is the best trip you have ever taken, and what made it so good?
  5. Are you more of a morning person or a night person, and has that always been true?
  6. What is something you are oddly good at?
  7. Who in your life makes you laugh the hardest?
  8. What does a perfect lazy Sunday look like for you?
  9. What is a film or book you wish you could experience again for the first time?
  10. What was your first job, and what did it teach you?
  11. Which of your friends have you known the longest?
  12. What is something you changed your mind about in the last few years?
  13. What is your favourite way to spend an evening alone?
  14. What smell takes you straight back to childhood?
  15. If you had a free afternoon tomorrow, what would you do with it?
  16. What is a tradition from your family that you would like to keep?
  17. What is the most spontaneous thing you have ever done?
  18. Which city or place feels most like home to you?
  19. What are you currently looking forward to?
  20. What do people usually get wrong about you at first?
  21. What is a skill you would love to learn if time were no issue?
  22. What kind of music did you love as a teenager?
  23. What is the kindest thing a stranger has ever done for you?
  24. How do you like to be cheered up after a rough day?
  25. What is one thing you would like me to know about you early on?

Take turns. Whoever asks listens without interrupting, then answers the same question. One question answered properly beats ten rushed ones.
