Gratitude questions for couples

Appreciation said out loud. Pick one before bed or over coffee; it takes two minutes and tends to change the mood of the whole day.

All 25 questions in this deck

  1. What is something I did this week that you appreciated?
  2. What is a quality of mine you rely on?
  3. When did I last make your day easier?
  4. What is something about our life right now that you are thankful for?
  5. Which everyday thing I do would you miss most?
  6. What is a way I have helped you grow?
  7. What part of our home life makes you happiest?
  8. Which friend or relative are we lucky to have?
  9. What is a recent moment between us that made you smile afterwards?
  10. What do I do that makes you feel understood?
  11. What is something difficult I handled in a way you admired?
  12. What is a small pleasure we share that you never get tired of?
  13. What have I taught you without meaning to?
  14. What is something you have never thanked me for?
  15. When did you last feel lucky to be with me?
  16. What about this past month are you most grateful for?
  17. What is a strength of ours as a couple?
  18. What do I say that reliably cheers you up?
  19. What is one thing your body or health let you do today?
  20. Which of my habits has quietly become one of your favourite things?
  21. What is something good that came out of a hard time?
  22. Who helped you this week, and how?
  23. What is a comfort we have now that we once only hoped for?
  24. What would you like to be thanked for more often?
  25. What is one thing about today that was better because we are together?

Take turns. Whoever asks listens without interrupting, then answers the same question. One question answered properly beats ten rushed ones.
