Questions about the future for couples

Where are you two heading? These help you compare maps before you are halfway down the road.

All 25 questions in this deck

  1. Where would you like us to be living in five years?
  2. What is one thing you want to have done before the end of next year?
  3. What does your ideal ordinary weekday look like a few years from now?
  4. Which trip do you most want us to take together?
  5. What is a dream you have not told many people about?
  6. How do you picture us celebrating our next big milestone?
  7. What would you like to be known for among the people closest to you?
  8. What new tradition would you like us to start?
  9. What is something you would like us to learn together?
  10. How do you imagine your working life changing over the next decade?
  11. What does being a good partner look like to you ten years in?
  12. What kind of home makes you feel most at ease?
  13. Which habits would you like us to build this year?
  14. What would you like more of in our life, and what would you like less of?
  15. If money were not a factor, how would you spend your days?
  16. What is a project you would love for us to take on together?
  17. How do you want us to handle it when our plans turn out to differ?
  18. What part of getting older are you looking forward to?
  19. What would make next year a great year for you?
  20. Which of your goals could I help with most?
  21. What place would you like to visit again with me?
  22. What do you hope never changes about us?
  23. How would you like us to spend holidays in the years ahead?
  24. What is one risk you would like us to take together?
  25. When you imagine us old, what are we doing?

Take turns. Whoever asks listens without interrupting, then answers the same question. One question answered properly beats ten rushed ones.
